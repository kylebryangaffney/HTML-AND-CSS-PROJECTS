Facts about the Saxophone

  1. It uses a reed to produce sound. This makes it a woodwind, not a brass instrument, even though it’s made of brass or sometimes bronze.

  2. Adolphe Sax onvented it in the 1840s. He designed it to blend the power of brass with the tone of woodwinds, which made it optimal for military and marching bands.

  3. It uses a conical-bore, unlike the other woodwind instruments. The body tube of a saxophone gradually widens, which can help players achieve a more even tone across registers, but requires more practice to do so.
